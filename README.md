# INSTRUMENT.Distortion

**Wiki:** [Documentation/README.md](Documentation/README.md) — principles, architecture, delivery, and the [SDK](Documentation/%F0%9F%A7%A9Manual%20and%20Artifacts/SDK.md).

The **Distortion** instruments of the APK.audio bench, as one thing: 2 models, their command tables and the family's panel. Audio analysers: a generator, distortion and level meters, and a scope view.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Is** | **inputs** — takes signals in — its connectors are inputs a patch can land on<br>**metering** — measures — its readings are numbers with units<br>**monitoring** — shows — a trace, a waveform or a display you watch<br>**source** — puts something out — a stimulus, a supply or a load |
| **Inputs and outputs** | every model's inputs and outputs — its connectors (from its BIM part), its commands' arguments and returns, and its bus topics — are on its SDK page: Documentation/🧩Manual and Artifacts/SDK/<Model>.md |
| **Recipe** | none — data, not a container: read in place by the stacks that bake `INSTRUMENTS` |
| **Source** | this repository (https://github.com/LikeDotAudio/APK.plugin.instruments.VISA.Distortion.git), a submodule of [APK.plugin.instruments.VISA](https://github.com/LikeDotAudio/APK.plugin.instruments.VISA) at `INSTRUMENT.Distortion/` |
| **Family name** | `Distortion` — the device type, the topic segment and the `manifest.json` key; the `INSTRUMENT.` prefix belongs to the folder only |
